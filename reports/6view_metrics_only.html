Key Findings

  • FL GS-LRM 5v (feed-forward) outperforms PS M5 (per-scene, 50 epochs) by +9.6 dB PSNR_fg.
  • Coverage gap (FL ~100% vs PS 89.3%) is the primary driver of PSNR_fg difference.
  • PSNR_intersection (pure color accuracy on overlap) shows FL still leads by ~3 dB.
  • FL E2E (MVDiff → GS-LRM) suffers from MVDiffusion bottleneck: ~10% transfer rate from GT performance.
