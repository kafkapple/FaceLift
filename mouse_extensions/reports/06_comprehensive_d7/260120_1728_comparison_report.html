2. Mathematical Foundation

2.1 Pinhole Camera Model

The projection from 3D world coordinates to 2D pixel coordinates:

\[ \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \sim \mathbf{K} \cdot [\mathbf{R} | \mathbf{T}] \cdot \begin{bmatrix} X \\ Y \\ Z \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

where \(\mathbf{K}\) is the intrinsic matrix:

\[ \mathbf{K} = \begin{bmatrix} f_x & 0 & c_x \\ 0 & f_y & c_y \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

2.2 Ray Direction Calculation

For novel view synthesis, we compute ray direction from pixel (u, v):

\[ \mathbf{d}_c = \begin{bmatrix} (u - c_x) / f_x \\ (v - c_y) / f_y \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

Critical: Wrong \(c_x, c_y\) → Wrong ray direction → Multi-view inconsistency

2.3 Scale Factor Calculations

D7 (fx_only):

\[ s = \frac{f_x^{target}}{f_x^{orig}}, \quad f_x' = 549, \quad f_y' = 549 \text{ (forced)} \]

D7.1 (individual):

\[ s_x = \frac{f_x^{target}}{f_x^{orig}}, \quad s_y = \frac{f_y^{target}}{f_y^{orig}}, \quad f_x' = f_y' = 549 \text{ (exact)} \]

D7.2 (average):

\[ s = \frac{s_x + s_y}{2}, \quad f_x' = f_x^{orig} \cdot s, \quad f_y' = f_y^{orig} \cdot s \]

2.4 PP-Centered Shift

After scaling, shift image to center PP at (256, 256):

\[ \Delta_x = 256 - c_x^{scaled}, \quad \Delta_y = 256 - c_y^{scaled} \]

The affine transform becomes:

\[ \begin{bmatrix} u' \\ v' \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} s_x & 0 \\ 0 & s_y \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} \Delta_x \\ \Delta_y \end{bmatrix} \]
