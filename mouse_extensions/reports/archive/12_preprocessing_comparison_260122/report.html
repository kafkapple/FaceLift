5. Recommendations

  • D1 (PP-Crop): Not recommended - per-view shifts cause cross-view inconsistency
  • D3 (Triangulation): Not normalized - requires additional normalization step
  • D7_1 (PP-Shift): Recommended - proper normalization with PP centering
  • D8 (Homography): Most precise - includes skew correction
